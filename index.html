Features: products with variants, pack units, batches and expiry dates; multi-store stock, transfers and stock counts; checkout, returns and customer notes; purchasing by barcode or by photographing the supplier's slip (recognized on the device); reports; Excel import/export; daily backups, optionally to your own Google Drive.
